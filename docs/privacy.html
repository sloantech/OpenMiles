Privacy and disclaimer

Last updated November 20, 2026. Applies to OpenMiles for Android.

Privacy: we collect nothing

OpenMiles has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere, and every build of the app checks that this stays true.

  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The developer never receives your data.
  • Location is used only to measure your drives, and only while a drive is recording. A notification shows whenever it is.
  • Background location and physical activity are only used if you turn on automatic drive detection, to notice when a drive starts and ends.
  • Everything stays on your phone, in the app's private storage. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for OpenMiles.
  • Exports (PDF, spreadsheet, backup file, detection log) only leave your phone when you tap Export, Share or Backup, and only go to the app or place you pick. What happens to them after that is up to that app or place.
  • Drive maps are drawn on your phone from the drive's saved GPS points. If you tap Open in Maps, the drive's start, end and a few points along the way are sent to Google Maps, which goes online to show streets. That only happens when you tap it, and Google's own privacy policy covers what Google Maps does.
  • Google Timeline imports are read on your phone. Only the car drives are kept.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Delete any backup files you saved, too.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic server logs of visits, as any web host does. The website itself uses no cookies, trackers or outside fonts.
